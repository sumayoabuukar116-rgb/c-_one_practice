Creating Variables
Variables are created to store the information entered by the user.
![creating variable](Screenshots/creatingvariable.png)

2.Assigning Variables
The values from the TextBoxes are assigned to variables.

The "Parse" method is used to convert the required Student ID value into the appropriate data type
![ASSIGNING VALUE](Screenshots/Assign_variable.png)

3.Concatenation

The "+" operator is used to combine different pieces of information into one string.
![Concatenation  string](Screenshots/using_concatination.png)

4.Display Output

The final information is displayed on the Label controls on the form.
![displaying output](Screenshots/displaying_output.png)

5.clearing TextBoxes

The "Clear()" method is used to remove the information entered in the TextBoxes
The Labels are also cleared so that the previous student information is removed from the form
After clicking the Clear button, the form becomes empty and ready for new information.
![clearing](Screenshots/clearing.png)
